About the Conference

"AI Convergence: Interdisciplinary Innovation through Intelligent Systems" Harnessing AI, ML, and DL for transforming Engineering, Science, Humanities, and Society

🎤 Objectives of the Conference


  • Promote cross-domain research integrating AI/ML/DL
  • Foster innovation through intelligent systems in diverse sectors
  • Encourage collaboration between academia, industry, and research institutions
  • Provide a platform to showcase emerging technologies, real-world applications, and responsible AI use

This conference serves as a platform for knowledge exchange, fostering collaborations, and showcasing cutting-edge research in AI and its convergence with various engineering disciplines.
